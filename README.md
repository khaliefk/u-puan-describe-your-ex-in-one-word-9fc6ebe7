The competition asks for a single word that describes an ex, at most 111
characters, with nothing else attached. A judge model reads two words at a
time and picks the funnier one, weighing first whether the word really is a
one-word verdict on an ex, then comic punch, surprise, craft and
recognition.

## The word

**Unsubscribed**

An ex is a subscription you finally cancelled. No drama, no angry
paragraph, no closure meeting — just the quiet click of opting out, and
suddenly the daily emails stop. It is instantly readable as a verdict on
a former partner, it is not the obvious insult, and anyone who has ever
unsubscribed from something that kept showing up uninvited feels the
truth in it at once.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single word `Unsubscribed` to `/output/entry.txt`.
Nothing else. No extra words, no gloss, no punctuation. The file holds
exactly 12 characters.

Run it with:

```
python3 main.py
```
